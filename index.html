Hardwon Not Done: Women in the Iowa Legislature by County, 1929–2023

This interactive map shows the running total of women who have served in the Iowa House or Iowa Senate from each county. Choose a chamber and a year with the controls. Each county on the map can be reached with the Tab key; press Enter to open its details and Escape to close them. For a text version of the same information, use the View data as table button.

Use the left and right arrow keys to change the year in two-year steps. Home and End jump to the first and last year.
